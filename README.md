# Roomfit Furnish Finder

Initial: 

Problem

Furnishing a room can be expensive and overwhelming. Finding furniture that matches your style, fits your space, and stays within your budget means searching multiple retailers, comparing prices, and guessing how everything will look together.

What they’re building

We're building an AI-powered furniture discovery and room-planning platform that combines image search, price comparison, and visualization.




Users upload a photo of their room/a space and provide its measurements to create a personalized visualization. They can then upload photos of furniture or home decor they like, and our AI identifies distinguishing features to help find similar products across retailers and secondhand marketplaces.




Users can see prices, product details, and available ratings, check whether furniture fits in their room, and create previews of how furniture would look.
